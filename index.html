What is your company going to make?
We've built an AI-native platform that reduces complex battery simulations from 7-8 days to under 30 minutes witnessed by e-TRNL energy, a battery company. The AI agent plans, builds, and runs scientific simulations from plain text. It converts a text prompt into an editable flowchart and clarifies constraints with the researcher before running the physical model on our compute. Researchers can instantly run parameter sweeps and visualize results, condensing weeks of complex scientific modeling into minutes of prompting so researchers can focus purely on the science.

Idea
Why did you pick this idea? Domain expertise?
We want to build a faster research ecosystem where the focus is science, not labor. We picked this because we've lived it — watching researchers lose weeks wrestling with clunky simulation tools. With current AI models, it's possible to do now what wasn't possible even 6 months ago. Both have domain expertise. Kunal lived the exact problem at ProtonCraft. Kamal scaled two startups as founding AI engineer with simulation interest. Customers like e-TRNL and Indi need not just software licenses but a specialist whose only work is translating science into software GUI.
Competitors? What do you understand that they don't?
COMSOL, ASPEN, ANSYS, SimScale — black boxes with decades-long update cycles. Godela, PhysicsX — mostly mechanical engineering, not energy R&D. Researchers can't see the physics, can't swap solvers, can't afford the licence. 80% of better open-source solvers sit unused because integration took weeks — until now. AI is finally good enough to orchestrate domain-specific simulations correctly. We're building at that inflection point — modular, transparent, pay-per-use. Starting with energy R&D where wrong answers cost years.
