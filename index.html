What We Build

We've built an AI-native platform that creates battery simulations in minutes, eliminating the 7-8 days it takes companies like e-TRNL Energy.

The AI agent plans, builds, and runs scientific simulations from plain text. It converts a text prompt into an editable flowchart and clarifies constraints with the researcher before running the physical model on our compute.

Researchers can instantly run parameter sweeps and visualize results, condensing weeks of complex scientific modeling into minutes of prompting so researchers can focus purely on the science.

The Problem

Every deep-tech team has the same problem. Researchers lose weeks wrestling with clunky simulation tools, writing boilerplate scripts, and stitching together workflows that should just work.

Incumbents like COMSOL, ASPEN, ANSYS, and SimScale are clusters of physics solvers wrapped in black boxes with decades-long update cycles. 80% of better open-source solvers sit unused because integration takes weeks.
